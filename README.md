## Gisele Andree, Ph.D. 👩‍🔬✨

Structural biologist and protein biochemist working to answer questions that at the intersection of protein complex regulation and a systems-driven view of how the cells adapt their proteomes.

Project Group Leader at the Max Planck Institute of Biochemistry (Schulman & Mann Labs), Munich.

## What I work on
- **De novo binder design:** designing protein-based probes computationally before experimental validation
- **Proteomics:** quantitative proteomics to profile cullin-RING E3 ligase activity in cells. Working to incorporate multiomic data to map how interactomes change in response to signals
- **Structural biology:** cryo-EM and mechanistic enzymology, from ribonucleotide reductases to ubiquitin ligases

Most of my research code lives in private lab repositories tied to unpublished work.
